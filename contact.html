Help center

Is ConWork free?

Yes, every feature is free. If you'd like to help us keep going, see the Donate page.

How do I create a team?

Sign up or sign in, choose Create team on the Teams page, give it a name and start a project.

How do I invite people?

Owners and admins can send email invites, or share the team code so colleagues can ask to join.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes, choose Continue with Google on the sign-in page. We only receive your name, email and profile picture.

Where is our data stored?

Sign-in is handled by Supabase, data and files are stored at Neon, and the site is served by Vercel. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

Email conwork.co@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we'll take care of it.
